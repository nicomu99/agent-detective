# Statement of Dr. Lionel Finch, physician

I have been the Blackwood family doctor for twelve years. After coffee I went up to my room in the guest wing at about five to ten and read until I heard the commotion. I did not leave my room and I did not go near the library.

Edmund's heart was weak. If digitalis was involved, perhaps he dosed himself; he knew I carried it. I keep my bag in my room, but the door does not lock, and anyone could have taken from it.

As for whatever Edmund said at dinner, I have no idea what he meant. We were on excellent terms.
