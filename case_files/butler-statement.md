# Statement of Albert Hargreaves, butler

At about 21:30 I passed the library and heard his lordship arguing with Miss Clara about money. He said: "You'll not see a penny more until you've learned some sense." Miss Clara came out at about 21:45 in tears.

At 22:15 I brought his lordship a freshly filled brandy decanter. He was writing a letter and said: "Not now, Hargreaves. This is a letter I should have written years ago." I left at once.

From 22:20 I polished the silver in the pantry. At 22:30 I took a tray of tea to Lady Vivian and the Colonel in the conservatory and was back in the pantry by 22:40. Mrs. Pike can tell you I did not leave again.

At 23:20 I went to collect the decanter and found his lordship dead. I noticed the library fire had been stoked up high, though I had not touched it since nine o'clock. I was in my usual black livery all evening.
