# Police search of the house, Sunday morning

Library: the brandy decanter and glass were taken for analysis; both contain digitalis. In the fireplace, under the ashes, were the half-burnt pages of a letter in Lord Blackwood's handwriting, addressed to the General Medical Council. The readable part says: "...can no longer stay silent about Dr. L. F., who has been writing prescriptions for morphine in the names of my tenants and selling the..." The rest is burnt.

Guest room of Dr. Finch: his black medical bag holds a bottle of digitalis tincture that is almost empty. His own ledger records that it was refilled on 10 November. A grey tweed jacket hung over the chair; there is a fresh brandy stain on its right cuff.

Room of Miss Clara: IOUs to a bookmaker totalling three hundred pounds.

Room of Colonel Ashby: a letter from Lord Blackwood, dated October, demanding repayment of five hundred pounds by Christmas.
