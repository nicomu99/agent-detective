# Floor plan of Blackwood Manor

Ground floor, east wing: the library sits at the far end of the east corridor. The back stairs start beside the library door and lead straight up to the guest wing.

Ground floor, west wing: the conservatory, at the far west end. Next to the main hall are the music room and, through a thin wall, the linen room.

Centre: the main hall with the grand staircase, the dining room and the drawing room.

Below stairs: the kitchen and the butler's pantry, across a passage from each other.

First floor: the family bedrooms over the west wing, and the guest wing over the east wing, where Dr. Finch and Colonel Ashby were staying. From the conservatory, the only way to the library is across the main hall and down the whole east corridor.
