# Statement of Colonel Rupert Ashby

Played cards with Lady Vivian in the conservatory from ten until the alarm. Never left the room; neither did she. The butler brought tea at half past ten.

I'll be frank: I owed Edmund five hundred pounds, and he had been pressing me for it. Not a motive for murder, Inspector. A gentleman settles his debts.

On my way to the conservatory, at about five to ten, I saw Finch going upstairs with that black medical bag of his. Said he was turning in early with a book. I wore my mess jacket all evening.
