# Statement of Lady Vivian Blackwood, wife

After coffee I went to the conservatory with Colonel Ashby at about ten o'clock, and we played bezique until we heard Hargreaves shouting at about 23:25. Neither of us left the room. Hargreaves brought us tea at half past ten.

My husband and I were not close these last years, I won't pretend otherwise. But at dinner he was in an odd mood. He told me he would "finally deal with that business with Lionel" tonight. He wouldn't say what he meant, and Dr. Finch went quite pale.
