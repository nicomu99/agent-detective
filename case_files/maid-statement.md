# Statement of Molly Reed, housemaid

From about 21:50 I was folding linen in the linen room, which is next door to the music room. Miss Clara was playing the piano the whole time, sad pieces, Chopin I think. She never stopped for longer than a minute or two until about eleven o'clock.

At about 22:40 I carried a pile of sheets up the back stairs, which start at the end of the east corridor, right by the library. The storm had blown out half the lamps. I saw a gentleman in a grey tweed jacket come out of the library carrying a small black bag. He hurried up the back stairs ahead of me towards the guest wing. I never saw his face.

I went back to the linen room after that and heard the piano again until eleven.
