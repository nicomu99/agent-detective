# Statement of Miss Clara Blackwood, niece

Yes, I argued with Uncle Edmund. I have debts, and he refused to help me again. I left the library at about a quarter to ten, very upset.

I went straight to the music room and played the piano to calm down. I played until about eleven and then went up to bed. Molly was in the linen room next door; she must have heard me.

I know I inherit the manor. Everybody knows. I was angry with him, but I would never have hurt him.
