# Case summary: the death of Lord Edmund Blackwood

Lord Edmund Blackwood, aged 61, was found dead in the library of Blackwood Manor at 23:20 on Saturday 14 November 1931. The butler, Albert Hargreaves, found him slumped over his writing desk with an empty brandy glass beside him.

A storm flooded the lane to the village that evening. Nobody arrived at or left the manor between 19:00 and the arrival of the police at 08:00 the next morning. Whoever did this was inside the house.

People in the house that night: Lady Vivian Blackwood (his wife), Clara Blackwood (his niece, 24), Dr. Lionel Finch (the family physician, staying for the weekend), Colonel Rupert Ashby (retired, an old army friend, staying for the weekend), Albert Hargreaves (butler), Mrs. Edna Pike (cook) and Molly Reed (housemaid).

The evening, as everyone agrees: dinner in the dining room from 19:30 to 21:15. Lord Blackwood then went to the library alone. The guests took coffee in the drawing room until about 21:50, when they went their separate ways. The alarm was raised at about 23:25.
