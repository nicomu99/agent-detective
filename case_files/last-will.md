# Summary of the last will, from the family solicitor

Miss Clara Blackwood inherits Blackwood Manor and the estate.

Lady Vivian Blackwood receives an annuity of two thousand pounds a year and the right to live at the manor for life.

Albert Hargreaves receives two hundred pounds "for long and loyal service".

All debts owed to Lord Blackwood are forgiven on his death, which includes the five hundred pounds owed by Colonel Ashby.

Dr. Lionel Finch is not mentioned in the will.
