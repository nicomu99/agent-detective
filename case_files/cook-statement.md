# Statement of Mrs. Edna Pike, cook

I was in the kitchen from seven until past midnight. The pantry door is right across from my table. Mr. Hargreaves opened a new, sealed bottle of brandy and filled the decanter there at about 22:10. He was in the pantry with his silver all evening, except for ten minutes or so when he took tea out at half past ten.

Earlier, at about half past six, before dinner, Dr. Finch came down to the kitchen. He asked which decanter his lordship drinks from in the evenings. "Doctor's orders," he said, "for his heart." I thought it odd, a doctor asking about brandy.

At dinner the gentlemen were a sight. Dr. Finch was in that grey tweed jacket of his; he never dresses for dinner, that one. The Colonel wore his old regimental mess jacket, red as a pillar box.
