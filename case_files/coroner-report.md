# Coroner's report

Cause of death: poisoning by digitalis, a heart drug made from the foxglove plant. A large dose was found in the stomach, in the dregs of the brandy glass on the desk, and in the brandy decanter itself.

Time of death: between 22:30 and 23:00. A dose this large acts within roughly fifteen to thirty minutes, so the poisoned brandy was most likely drunk between 22:15 and 22:45.

Lord Blackwood had a weak heart, but he had never been prescribed digitalis. His own physician would know the drug and the dose well; an ordinary person would not.

A bruise on the left temple matches the corner of the writing desk. He struck it as he collapsed. There are no signs of a struggle and no other injuries.
